CareBridge, end to end

Discharge papers go in. A daily voice check goes out. Danger goes to the nurse. Everything lands in one record that the nurse, the doctor and the mom can all see.

The danger check, exactly

ResultMomBabyWhat happens
Red · 911Chest pain, can't breathe, seizure, thoughts of self-harmCan't wake up, not moving, very weakTell mom to call 911 now. Nurse alert.
Red · urgent160+ top or 110+ bottom. Or 140–159 / 90–109 with headache, vision change, upper belly painFever 100.4°F+, feeding poorly, yellow skin spreading, no pee 8+ hoursTell mom to go to the ER or call her doctor now. Nurse alert at once.
Yellow150–159 top or 100–109 bottom, no symptoms. Or rising 3 days in a rowFewer than 6 wet diapers after day 5, yellow eyesNurse reviews today. Doctor gets the trend.
GreenUnder 150/100, no symptomsNormal feeds and diapersSaved to the record. Next call tomorrow.

Numbers come from the SMFM checklist, the Mississippi toolkit, POST-BIRTH and AAP. The hospital can change them. The nurse makes every medical decision.
